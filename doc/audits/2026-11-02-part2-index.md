## Verdict

The documentation problem is **not missing content**. All 24 modules are
documented, every MCP tool parameter is specified, the codemap is fresh and
test-enforced, and the probe/smoke reports contain real measured evidence.
The problem is that **758 KB of prose and 4.2 MB of evidence have no
organisation**: 28 prose files sit flat in root/`docs/` with no index, 28 of 31
relative links are broken, six kinds of document share one folder, and the same
cleanup rule is written three times (SPEC §23, D88–D100, CHANGELOG). The
biggest body of recent work — `cleanup_jobs` — has the **least** committed
evidence: no `docs/reports/cleanup_*` directory and 16 MB of uncommitted
review material under `~/agy-review/`.

The target is a plain-markdown `doc/` with `using/`, `reference/`, `design/`,
`findings-and-experiments/`, `audits/`, `project/`, `release-notes/` and
`developing/`; numbered experiment pages; dated audits; a `## Sources` footer
on every page; and one generated checker. No site generator is needed now.
